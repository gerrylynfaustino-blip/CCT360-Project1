Character Design: Bringing Imagination to Life

An exploration of character illustration, creative expression, and the process of translating an original idea onto a canvas.

Project Overview

Inspiration

This digital illustration features an original character designed to explore personality, expression, and visual storytelling. Through a combination of sketching, colour, and rendering techniques, I wanted to create a character with a distinct appearance and expressive personality.

Creative Process

I used Pinterest to gather artistic references for my original character, focusing on poses, art styles, and rendering techniques. These references helped me explore different approaches to character illustration while maintaining a consistent visual style throughout my artwork.

Final Result

The final illustration presents a playful and expressive character through her pose, facial expression, and warm colour palette. The loose linework and soft rendering give the artwork a stylized appearance, while the yellow clothing and ribbon help establish a recognizable character design.
